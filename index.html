Analyst decision framework

Why does the primary rating hold?

The rating is not decided by one ratio. Valuation sets the entry discipline, then profitability, earnings direction, financial quality and dividends confirm or weaken the case. For banks, insurers, investment and real-estate companies, P/B is read together with ROE. For operating companies, P/E is read more heavily alongside EPS growth and ROE.
